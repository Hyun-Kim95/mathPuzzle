# Privacy Policy — mathPuzzle (draft)

**Last updated:** 2026-05-18  
**Status:** Draft — host at a public URL before store release.

## Summary

mathPuzzle is an offline logic puzzle game. We do not require an account. Progress is stored on your device.

## Data we process

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Game progress & settings | Local gameplay (device storage) |
| Advertising (Google AdMob) | Rewarded videos for optional hints/answers |
| In-app purchases | Optional tips to support the developer |

We do **not** offer in-app contact in v1. We do **not** collect email unless you contact us outside the app.

## Children

The app targets a general audience including children. We configure ads for child-directed treatment and use consent flows before showing ads.

## Your choices

You can reset progress in Settings. You can decline ad consent; hints/answers that require ads will not unlock.

## Third parties

- Google AdMob (ads)
- Google Play (purchases on Android)

See their privacy policies for details.
